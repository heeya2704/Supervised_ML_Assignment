# Session 15 - Task 1: GradientBoostingClassifier on Iris Dataset

## Task Overview
Train a **Gradient Boosting Classifier (`GradientBoostingClassifier`)** on the Iris dataset and evaluate its classification accuracy on the test set.

---

## Model Hyperparameters & Results
- **Algorithm**: `sklearn.ensemble.GradientBoostingClassifier`
- **Boosting Stages (`n_estimators`)**: `100`
- **Learning Rate (`learning_rate`)**: `0.1`
- **Loss Function**: Multinomial deviance (Cross-Entropy)
- **Dataset Split**: 105 training samples / 45 test samples
- **Test Accuracy**: **93.33%**
